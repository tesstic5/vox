## Part 1 — Defects (things that are wrong today)

### D1 · The diary is on a UTC clock in the default view — dragging moves appointments 4–7 hours
**Severity: high.** This is the receptionist's main screen, and the default is "All sites".
**✅ Fixed 2026-10-15** — `diaryRange` resolves one clock (`practiceTimezone`: the filtered site, else the first site by name), buckets days with DST-safe local midnights, measures every row against that same `dayStart`, and returns it as `timezone` with `gridSiteId`. The payload is drawn and dragged on one clock. Verified: a row's drawn position rebuilds to its real instant, a drop lands where it was dropped, and both site views agree.

`lib/server/diary.ts:96` — `const timezone = location?.timezone ?? 'UTC';` — with no site filter the payload says UTC. The comment three lines above says *"the practice's first site's timezone"*, so the code and its own documentation disagree. Three consequences, all measured:

```
ALL-SITES view → payload timezone: UTC     (sites are America/New_York and America/Los_Angeles)
a drop at 14:00 on the grid sends 2026-10-20T14:00Z
   → it landed at 10:00 New York / 07:00 San Francisco        ← 4 hours from where it was dropped
a 20:30 New York appointment (00:30Z next day):
   all-sites view draws it on: (not on 2026-10-20 at all)     ← the wrong day column
   one-site view  draws it on: 2026-10-20 at 20:30            ← correct
```

- **Rows** are drawn in their own site's local minutes (`diary.ts:139`, `const zone = site?.timezone ?? timezone`) — so the blocks look right.
- The page's drag math trusts `data.timezone` (`app/dashboard/calendar/page.tsx:259`), which is UTC → the appointment lands 4–7 hours away.
- **Day columns** are bucketed on UTC midnight (`diary.ts:125`), so anything after 19:00 New York or after 16:00 San Francisco appears on the next day.

**Fix shape:** without a site filter, resolve the window from the practice's primary site (or bucket each column in that column's site timezone) and return the *row's* zone to the UI so drag math uses the same clock the block was drawn on. The P8 gate missed this because it moved appointments through the API, where the math is correct.

### D2 · The reminder says "Reply C to confirm" — and the reply does nothing
**Severity: high** — a promise in the product's own copy that the product ignores.
**✅ Fixed 2026-10-15** — `lib/server/inbound.ts` reads four intents (confirm · decline · change · question); confirm and decline go through the desk's own `setAppointmentStatus`, a change request is filed for a person and never guessed at. Verified: `Body=C` → `Confirmed — Thursday 15 October at 9:00am …`, appointment `confirmed`.

`lib/server/templates.ts:148` (seeded, enabled): `"Hi {{client_first_name}} — a reminder: your {{appt}} is {{when}} … Reply C to confirm or call {{business}} to change it."`

`app/api/telephony/twilio/inbound-message/route.ts` understands `STOP`/`UNSUBSCRIBE`/`CANCEL`/`END`/`QUIT` and `START`/`UNSTOP`. Everything else: *"recorded, not answered"*. Proven:

```
probe appointment: bkg_7a8e134b755f92ee | status pending
webhook replied: <Response></Response>
appointment after replying "C": pending      ← the client believes they confirmed
messages sent back: 0
```

The client thinks they have confirmed; the practice's diary still says pending. Two more seeded templates make the same unkept promise: the confirmation ("reply to this message … to change it") and the recall ("Reply with a day and we will book you in").

**Fix shape:** handle the replies the copy already invites — `C`/`CONFIRM`/`Y`/`YES`/`1` → confirm the next appointment for that number; a date-shaped reply → queue a booking request for the desk (and answer so the client knows it landed). Small, and it turns the outbound copy into a feature.

### D3 · Password reset, magic link, verify-email and invite are theatre
**Severity: high for a real practice** — a solo owner who forgets their password is locked out permanently.
**✅ Fixed 2026-10-15** — all five flows are real: password reset (request + confirm), magic link, the six-digit verify code, and invitations, on a hashed-at-rest token table (`lib/server/auth-tokens.ts`) with `scripts/auth_check.py` as the standing gate. And the sentence three screens print — *"an unverified account can build agents but cannot publish or provision a number"* — is now enforced by `requireVerifiedEmail` on publish, number purchase and API keys, which is why the fix went further than the four screens: the copy and the code now say the same thing. What it costs and what it refuses is in `AUDIT-D3-AUTH-2026-10-15.md`.

Every one of those screens has **zero API calls** (`grep -c "request(" ` on each: 0). `components/site/AuthFlows.tsx:245` sets React state, toasts *"Reset link sent."*, and offers a hard-coded fake token: `ctaHref="/reset-password/rt_9f42c1ab"`. The login screen also advertises `/invite/inv_7c2f91ab`. There is no `/api/auth/reset`, no token minting, no verification step, and nothing anywhere gates on `emailVerified`.

The *real* recovery path that does exist is owner/admin → Team → **Reset password**, which mints a real one-time password (and ends their sessions). What is missing is the self-serve half — and the marketing surfaces claim it's there.

**Fix shape:** mint a real single-use token, send it through the transport that already exists (`transports.ts`, `VOXERA_EMAIL_API_BASE`), give it a real `/reset-password/[token]` handler, and either make `/verify-email` do something or stop linking to it from the product.

### D4 · A payment sends no receipt
`lib/server/money.ts` never queues a message: no `queueMessage`, no `confirmationBody`, no receipt concept. `recordPayment` writes the row and stops. The client pays and hears nothing. (The assistant's generic support path even says *"A receipt is on its way"* — which is only true for the fictional support flow.)

### D5 · Overdue invoices have no chase loop
`money.ts:1193` computes what is overdue for the desk, and the public pay link exists — but the scheduler runs only `deliver-messages · queue-reminders · no-show-followups · visit-followups · campaign-tick · retention`. Nothing ever chases a balance. Money stops at "we billed it".

### D6 · The wait list is matched, then ignored
**✅ Fixed 2026-10-15** — a freed slot is now *offered*: the desk (or the cancellation itself, from any of four doors: the status route, the client's own link, an inbound SMS, or a series delete) offers the time to the people whose window it falls inside, by name, by text or email, with the client's own single-use link. One tap books it; a refusal is honest and carries the live alternatives so the tap has somewhere to go; declining keeps them on the list; expiry (4 h, capped at the appointment's own start) puts them back in the queue. At most 5 offers stand on one slot. See `AUDIT-D6-WAITLIST-2026-10-15.md`, gated by `scripts/waitlist_check.py` (77 checks) and `scripts/offer_expiry_probe.ts` (8, offline).

*As the audit found it:* `lib/server/diary.ts:398` returned the matching wait-list entries when a cancellation freed a slot; both screens rendered them as a toast (`calendar/page.tsx:299`). Nothing offered the freed time to the people who asked for it. The most valuable minute in a clinic — a cancelled 3pm on a full day — was left to a receptionist's memory.

### D7 · Recalls are a count with no action, and the interval is one number for the whole practice
`practiceProfile.recallDays` is a single value (default 180) applied to every service (`reports.ts:625`), so an annual service and a 6-month checkup are recalled on the same schedule. The report lists who is overdue; there is no "send the recall" — no queueing, no campaign, no link.

### D8 · Documentation stops at P4, and there is no single command that proves the whole thing
`lib/docs-content/` covers `api/booking · messaging · practice · reports` (plus the platform pages). Nothing covers notes, invoices and payments, team·roles, the staff board, import/export — or `practice_report`, the tool I added last phase. And the suite is 14 files run by hand: `package.json` has no `check` script.
